The garden wakes up slowly in the spring. First the snowdrops, then the crocus,
then the tulips that the neighbour gave us. The soil is cold and the mornings are
wet, but the garden does not mind. It has waited all winter for the light.

We plant the beans in May and the tomatoes in June. The beans climb the fence and
the tomatoes lean on their canes. By August the garden is loud with bees and the
kitchen smells of basil. The garden gives more than we can eat, so we share it.
