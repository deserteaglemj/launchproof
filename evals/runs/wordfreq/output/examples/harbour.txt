The harbour is quiet before the boats go out. The fishermen check the nets and
the gulls wait on the wall. When the tide turns the boats leave one by one, and
the harbour is empty until the evening.

In the evening the boats come back with the light behind them. The gulls follow
the boats in, the nets come up on the quay, and the harbour is loud again. Nobody
says much. It's been the same for a hundred years, and it'll be the same tomorrow.
